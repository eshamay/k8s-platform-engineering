# Port

Port is a commercial SaaS developer portal. It models the catalog as blueprints and entities, adds self-service actions, and integrates with Kubernetes, Argo CD, GitHub, and cloud providers. [sources/research-landscape](../sources/research-landscape.md)

## Positioning

Port's own getting-started page positions it against [entities/backstage](backstage.md) as more automation and AI-agent oriented (https://docs.port.io/getting-started/overview). The dossier labels this marketing framing, and no independent comparison was found. The wiki therefore records the claim as vendor positioning. See [findings/landscape-comparisons-of-argo-cd-flux-port-and-kratix-come-from-vendors-with-no-independent-benchmarks](../findings/landscape-comparisons-of-argo-cd-flux-port-and-kratix-come-from-vendors-with-no-independent-benchmarks.md).
