# The Cozystack CNCF Sandbox application issue 87 was closed on 2024-08-20 over governance and adopter concerns

## Scenario

Cozystack, a project of Aenix, applied to the CNCF Sandbox in issue 87 of the cncf/sandbox repository. The issue was closed on 2024-08-20 with the words "Closing this application in favor of a future application to sandbox". The dossier summarizes the TOC concerns as business separation, adopters, maintainer diversity, and governance. [[sources/research-gap-o5-kro-ecosystem]] URL: https://github.com/cncf/sandbox/issues/87.

## Why it matters

Aenix is the only voice proposing the Kubernetes-as-a-kernel distribution model, so the neutral-governance status of its flagship project bears on how much independent weight the proposal carries. The closure is evidence about Cozystack's governance in 2024, and the dossier did not check whether a later application was filed.
