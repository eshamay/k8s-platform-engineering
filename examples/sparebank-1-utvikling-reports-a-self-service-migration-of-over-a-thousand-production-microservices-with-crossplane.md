# SpareBank 1 Utvikling reports a self-service migration of over a thousand production microservices with Crossplane

## Scenario

SpareBank 1 Utvikling lists itself in the Crossplane ADOPTERS file with a production migration. [[sources/research-gap-g1-named-adopters]]

## What the entry says

The entry describes a self-service "migration of over a thousand production microservices, databases and caches" to EKS and AWS managed services. It is undated and self-reported. URL: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crossplane/crossplane/main/ADOPTERS.md.

## How to read it

Unlike the pure resource counts in other entries, this one counts workloads that moved, so it describes the platform's reach and not the number of managed-resource objects. It is one of few entries that says "production" with a number attached.
