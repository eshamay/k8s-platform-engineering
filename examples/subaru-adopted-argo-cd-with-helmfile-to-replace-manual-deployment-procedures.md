# Subaru adopted Argo CD with Helmfile to replace manual deployment procedures

## Scenario

CNCF publishes a case study of Subaru, headed "Adopting GitOps with Argo CD", at https://www.cncf.io/case-studies/subaru/. It describes a move from manual deployment procedures and Helm scripts to Argo CD combined with Helmfile. The account is self-reported by Subaru through CNCF. The dossier read only keyword-filtered excerpts and not the full page, and the excerpts carry no date. [[sources/research-gap-g1-gitops-drift-incidents]]

## What the dossier found

The case study reports no outage and no incident, and the dossier recorded no scale figures from it. It is a named end-user adoption story and not a convergence or failure story.

## Why it belongs here

It is one of the few named, vendor-neutral GitOps adopters in the wiki, next to [[examples/the-argo-cd-users-file-lists-452-self-reported-adopters]] and [[examples/morgan-stanley-runs-flux-across-more-than-500-clusters-with-self-service-onboarding]].
