# Consensys reports managing more than 1,000 resources with Crossplane

## Scenario

Consensys lists itself in the Crossplane ADOPTERS file with a resource count and a link to a KubeCon talk. [[sources/research-gap-g1-named-adopters]]

## What the entry says

The entry says the company is "managing more than 1,000 resources". It is undated and self-reported. The dossier did not watch the linked talk, so no detail beyond the one-liner is recorded here. URL: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/crossplane/crossplane/main/ADOPTERS.md.

## How to read it

The count is modest next to the 10,000 and 200,000 figures elsewhere in the same file, and the talk link makes it the most likely entry to yield a fuller first-party account. The wiki treats the talk as unread until someone watches it.
