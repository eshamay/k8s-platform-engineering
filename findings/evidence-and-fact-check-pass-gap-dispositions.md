## What the pass changed

The pass fixed a set of version-sensitive facts that earlier pages carried as unverified. Crossplane v2.5 scope and claims handling, the status of deletionPolicy, managementPolicies and Operations, and the activate-everything default for activation policies are now sourced. The same holds for kro v0.10.0 tuning defaults and conditions, the Flux and Argo CD interval defaults, Requeue true backoff, Mutating Admission Policy and StorageVersionMigration stages, the Flux pre-dry-run flag, and Argo CD sharding labels. The scale recheck changed wording and not numbers. Across the 49 gaps the proposed counts are 22 resolved-candidates (21 in group 3 and one in group 1), 23 partials and 4 still-open.

Group 1 produced far less. Two primary postmortems were read in full (OpenAI December 2024 and Jetstack 2019) and Neon's 2025 report was found, and several reproduction-backed issues now back the leaked-resource, adoption, status-bug and reconcile-loop findings.
