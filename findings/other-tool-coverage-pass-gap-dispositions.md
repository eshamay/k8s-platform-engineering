# Other-tool coverage pass gap dispositions

> [!NOTE]
> This page is a triage aid. It proposes a disposition for each of 35 open gaps after the nine o1 to o9 research dossiers were ingested (commits `3cd0173` through `e3208ff`, seq 67 to 75). No gap was closed and `open-knowledge-gaps.md` was not edited by this page. Each gap already carries a "Possible resolution" note from the ingest, and only the user closes gaps, through `sticki-gaps`. Dispositions are one reader's judgment of the dossier evidence, and the dossiers read documentation on `main` or master, not pinned release tags.

Disposition key: **resolved-candidate** means the evidence answers the gap well enough that the user could close it. **partial** means progress with a named remainder. **still-open** means nothing usable was found. **needs-different-source** means the answer exists only in material the tools could not read.

Counts: 11 resolved-candidate, 19 partial, 5 still-open, 0 needs-different-source. No gap is classed needs-different-source because every unreadable source was a plausible but unconfirmed location of an answer, not a known one. They are listed under "Needs manual fetch" so the user can decide.

The nine dossiers moved most of the 35 gaps from "unknown" to "documented from a primary source".
