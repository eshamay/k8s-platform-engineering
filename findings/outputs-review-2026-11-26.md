## Scope and method

The review covered all seven outputs, which together hold about 137,000 words. Each document was read by four independent reviewers. One reviewer checked citation fidelity by opening every cited wiki page and comparing the sentence against it. A second reviewer swept for claims resting on pages the wiki marks contradicted, superseded, stale or draft. A third checked completeness and currency against the rest of the wiki. A fourth checked the timeless-voice and prose contract. A fixer then opened the wiki page behind each reported issue, applied the real ones in place and recorded the rest as skipped or as needing a decision, and a verifier checked the edited document against the wiki. Two cross-document passes followed, one for consistency of shared facts and one for dangling links, history wording and banned words. The review changed only the seven outputs. The lists below describe each change by location, grouped where several edits in one section share a purpose. The wiki pages the review found to be wrong or inconsistent were not edited.
